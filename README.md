🚀 STARVIS AI – Academic Helper & Study Companion


📚 Smart Academic Management Powered by AI
Track assignments, manage tasks, plan study sessions, monitor productivity, and get AI-powered academic assistance — all in one place.


📖 Overview
STARVIS AI is an intelligent academic productivity platform designed specifically for students.
It combines:
🤖 AI-powered academic assistance
📅 Academic calendar management
✅ Smart task and to-do tracking
📚 Assignment management
📊 Study analytics
🎯 Goal tracking
🔔 Study reminders & notifications
🔐 Secure Google Authentication
The goal is simple:
Help students stay organized, improve consistency, and achieve better academic performance through intelligent planning and analytics.
